What the assistant can and cannot do

Read-only
It can search and read your archive and start a refresh. It cannot send, edit or delete anything in Telegram.
Local
The archive stays on this computer. Messages reach the AI service only when the assistant reads them to answer you.
Careful with chat text
Messages are marked as untrusted, so instructions hidden in a chat are not followed as commands.
